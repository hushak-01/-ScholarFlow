# First-use onboarding

Ask only missing items: topics/equations, current projects, bilingual keywords, important authors/journals/seed papers, methods/exclusions, date window, and digest size.

Defaults: Zotero; local TeXstudio; review before saving; weekly digest of 5 papers; shareable `literature-wiki/` and local-private `private/`.

Confirm only assumptions that affect the current run. Deliver a sample before forcing a long questionnaire.
